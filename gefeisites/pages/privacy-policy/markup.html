Privacy policy

Updated 19 November 2026

You can run every screen test without an account. Here is what happens to your test results and what the site receives when you visit.

What stays on your device

The touch test uses your taps and swipes to calculate grid coverage and count simultaneous touches. The guided check uses the answers you choose to build its summary. Both run in your browser. Downloading a touch report saves the file to your device. ScreenTestHub does not receive a copy of the report, your touch paths or your guided answers.

Your language preference

When you choose a language, the site saves your choice in a cookie called screen_test_locale for up to one year. You can remove it in your browser’s settings for this site.

Page requests and traffic statistics

Cloudflare delivers the pages and receives connection information, including your IP address, when your browser requests a page or image. The site uses Ahrefs Web Analytics to measure visits, referral sources and popular pages. Ahrefs describes its analytics as cookie-free. The hosting engine can also record page views and interactions through PVUV.

Your browser controls

You can clear this site’s cookies and storage or block analytics requests in your browser. Downloaded reports stay in your downloads folder until you remove them.

Contact us about privacy

Send privacy questions to privacy@screentesthub.com. Cloudflare forwards your message to our inbox. We use your email address and the information you share to respond to your request.
