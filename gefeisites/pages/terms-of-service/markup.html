Use these tests to look for a display or touch problem and keep a record of what you see.

Running a test

You choose when to start a test, switch patterns or enter fullscreen. Use your normal viewing distance and a comfortable brightness. Stop a moving or changing pattern whenever you wish. Use the on-screen controls or your browser’s fullscreen controls to return to the page.

Interpreting a result

A mark that appears on several solid colors is worth checking again. Clean the screen, note the colors involved and repeat under the same conditions. Browser zoom, display settings and room lighting can change what you see. A result alone cannot confirm a hardware fault or establish warranty coverage.

Sharing a result or arranging a repair

You can share a link to a test or your downloaded touch report with a technician or seller. Review the file before sending it. The seller or manufacturer decides how to handle your return or repair under the terms that apply to your device. These terms do not limit your statutory consumer rights.
